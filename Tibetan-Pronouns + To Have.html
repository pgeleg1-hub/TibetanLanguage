Understanding Tibetan Possessives

In Tibetan, how you say you have something depends on whether it's always yours, something you just noticed right now, or something you don't have at all!

General Possession

ང་ ལ་ དེབ་ ཡོད།

"Nga la deb yod"

I have a book (general fact).

Use ཡོད། (Yod) when you normally have it all the time.

Non-Possession

ང་ ལ་ དེབ་ མེད།

"Nga la deb med"

I don't have a book.

Use མེད། (Med) when you don't have it at all.

Realised / Noticed

ང་ ལ་ དེབ་ འདུག

"Nga la deb dug"

I have a book (realised/noticed).

Use འདུག (Dug) when you just found it, realised it or noticed it right now.

Realised / Noticed Non-Possession

ང་ ལ་ དེབ་ མི་འདུག

"nga la deb mi-dug"

I don't have a book (realised/noticed).

Use མི་འདུག (mi-dug) when you just realised/noticed you don't have it.

💡 Grammar Breakdown Formula

ང་ལ་ (Nga la) [To me / In my possession] + [Object] + ཡོད / འདུག / མེད
